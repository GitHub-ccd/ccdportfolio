# AI Model Selection & Tooling Guide

This reference document explains the model tiers in Google's Gemini AI family, helping you select the right model for speed, cost efficiency, and coding capabilities.

---

## ⚡ Google Gemini Model Tier Breakdown

| Model Tier | Cost Tier | Speed | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gemini Flash** *(Recommended)* | 💰 **Low Cost (Budget Friendly)** | ⚡ Extremely Fast | Web development, coding HTML/CSS/JS/React, writing documentation, everyday pair programming. |
| **Gemini Flash (High)** | 💰💰 Moderate | ⚡ Fast | Complex multi-file coding, detailed technical planning, and structured refactoring. |
| **Gemini Pro** | 💰💰💰💰 **High Cost (Flagship)** | 🐢 Slower / Deep Reasoning | Advanced mathematical proofs, complex algorithmic research, or massive enterprise codebase refactors. |

---

## 🔑 Key Takeaway: Flash vs. Pro

* **"Flash" = Fast & Economical:** Flash models are specifically designed to be lightweight, high-speed, and cost-optimized. They are up to **5x–10x less expensive per token** than Pro models while retaining strong coding intelligence.
* **"Pro" = Flagship & Costly:** Pro models are Google's largest, most expensive reasoning models designed for heavy computation.

---

## 💡 Recommendation for Portfolio Development

For building, customizing, and deploying your portfolio website:
* **Default Choice:** Use **Gemini 3.6 Flash** (or Flash High). It provides fast responses, low cost, and excellent accuracy for all web development tasks.
