# Portfolio Examples & Benchmark Strategy

## 🎯 Strategic Roadmap: The Portfolio Evolution Model

This document outlines the benchmark models for our portfolio website, defining where we start today (**Phase 1: Jessie-Raye Bauer Model**) and where we are heading long-term (**Phase 2: Tim Hopper Model**).

---

## 📍 Phase 1 (Now): The Jessie-Raye Bauer Model
*Reference: [Jessie-Raye Bauer's Portfolio](https://jessierayebauer.wixsite.com/jrbauer)*

### Core Philosophy: "Data Storytelling & Clear Narrative"
Rather than being just a cold collection of code links, this model treats data science projects as **narrative case studies**. It showcases the human behind the data, demonstrating strong domain knowledge, communication, and business/clinical impact.

### Key Structural Pillars:
1. **Engaging "About Me":**
   * Clear personal story connecting background/education to healthcare data science.
   * Approachable professional photo, concise bio, and direct links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and CV.
2. **Blog-Style Applied Case Studies:**
   * **Format:** Instead of dumping raw code, each project is written like an insightful, easy-to-read case study or lab notebook entry.
   * **Content Examples:**
     * *Personal/API Data Analysis:* E.g. analyzing health wearable metrics or clinical trial datasets.
     * *Practical Statistical / ML Guides:* Explaining how a specific model was applied to solve a healthcare challenge.
     * *Business & Clinical Impact:* Clearly articulating *why* the project matters to patients, clinicians, or healthcare managers.
3. **Publications & Research:**
   * Structured list of academic contributions, abstracts, or clinical reports with direct PDF links.
4. **Clean, Readable Layout:**
   * Uncluttered typography, spacious padding, intuitive navigation tabs (Home, Projects, Articles, Publications, About/Contact).

---

## 🚀 Phase 2 (Aspiration): The Tim Hopper Model
*Reference: [Tim Hopper's Site](https://tdhopper.com/)*

### Core Philosophy: "Technical Thought Leadership & Ecosystem Hub"
This model represents an established senior domain authority. The website expands beyond an individual resume into a rich personal hub featuring open-source tools, podcasts, community resources, and opinionated technical writing.

### Key Structural Pillars to Grow Into:
1. **Resource Hub & Toolkits:**
   * Independent guides and cheat sheets (e.g. Python tooling guides, healthcare dataset handbooks, pipeline templates).
2. **Multi-Format Content:**
   * Podcast episodes, video walkthroughs, or interview series (e.g. discussions on health AI ethics or careers).
3. **Opinionated Technical Essays:**
   * High-level articles on ML infrastructure, data engineering best practices, and industry trends.
4. **Personal Knowledge Base:**
   * Digital garden / curated notes that position you as an expert and mentor in the field.

## 🏛️ Website Architecture (Designed for Both Phases)

To ensure the new website scales smoothly from **Phase 1 (Jessie-Raye Bauer)** to **Phase 2 (Tim Hopper)** without needing major rewrites, we build a modular architecture:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        MAIN NAVIGATION BAR                             │
│  [Logo / Name]   Home   About   Projects/Case Studies   Articles   CV  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  HERO SECTION                                                          │
│  Hi, I'm [Name] — Healthcare Data Scientist & AI Researcher.            │
│  Bridging data science, clinical insights, and healthcare innovation.  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  FEATURED NARRATIVE CASE STUDIES (Jessie-Raye Bauer Style)              │

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

```

## 📋 Action Plan for Content Creation

1. **Craft the Bio / Hero:** Write a compelling, 2-paragraph introduction highlighting your background in healthcare data analytics.
2. **Select 2–3 Initial Case Studies:** Structure each around:
   * *Problem & Clinical Context*
   * *Data & Methodology*
   * *Results & Key Takeaways*
   * *Code & Interactive Demo Link*
3. **Prepare Publications & CV Section:** Format your papers, posters, and resume for easy inline viewing or PDF download.
